About Sajora

Sajora Collective is a women's boutique in Ludhiana, run by Kuljinder Kaur and Charanjeet Kaur.

We bring traditional Indian ethnic wear and contemporary everyday fashion together on one rack: statement prints and bordered dupattas beside modern silhouettes you can wear again and again.

Not sure what suits you or what goes with what? Ask us. Outfit coordination is part of the shop.

Clothes that feel premium, look feminine and still work for a regular Tuesday.
